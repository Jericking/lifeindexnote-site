Ghost Mode

Social battery at zero but the group chat won't stop buzzing? Ghost replies for you and helps you slip away gracefully.

Try it as a testerGoogle Play

Features

Everything for the days you can't text back.

Auto-reply

Replies straight from the notification Reply action in the apps you choose: KakaoTalk, Discord, Slack, SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram and more.

Context-aware replies

Tells urgent messages, questions and casual chats apart, with an optional "(auto-reply)" tag.

Social battery

Shows your remaining social energy from today's messages and check-ins (optionally calendar and screen time).

Auto Ghost

Turns on by itself when your battery drops below a level you set.

Graceful exit

Fake incoming call, fake urgent message, tap-to-copy excuses.

Privacy

All data stays on your phone, never sent to a server. No accessibility service.

Free / Premium

Free

5 auto-replies per day, with banner ads

Premium (one-time purchase)

Unlimited auto-replies, your own reply lines, no ads
