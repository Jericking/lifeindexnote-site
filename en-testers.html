Try our apps before launch

Google Play requires 12 testers to keep a new app installed for 14 days before it can launch publicly. Try both apps for free and tell us what you think.

How to join

All you need is an Android phone and a Google account. It takes about two minutes.

  1. Join the Google GroupUsing the Google account you'll test with, join the tester group (one group for both apps).
  2. Opt inTap "Become a tester" on each page:
    Kaltoe Defense · Ghost Mode
  3. Install from Google PlayUse the Play Store link on the opt-in page. It can take a few minutes to show up.
  4. Keep it for 14 daysOpen it now and then, and send feedback via the Play Store's private feedback or email.

FAQ

Does it cost anything?

No, installing and testing are free. In-app purchases are real charges, so only buy if you want to.

"Item not found" in Play Store

Make sure the account you used for the group and opt-in is the one signed in to the Play Store, then try again in a few minutes.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android only for now.

How do I stop testing?

Tap "Leave the program" on the opt-in page and uninstall the app.
