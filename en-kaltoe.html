Kaltoe Defense

It's Friday, 5:59 PM, and your boss walks over: "Just one more thing before you go…" "Kaltoe" is Korean slang for leaving work right on time. Merge sticker-style office characters and clock out at 6 PM sharp.

Try it as a testerGoogle Play

Screenshots show the Korean UI; the game is also available in English and Japanese.

Features

(Any resemblance to your actual manager is purely coincidental. Probably.)

Summon + merge = promotion

Drag two identical coworkers together to promote them, then pick one of 3 candidates.

7 company chapters

Job-hop from a general company to a conglomerate. Beat the Friday boss to move on; endless Overtime Mode after.

A new boss every weekday

Stun, shields, rage, summons, transforms, splits: bosses get nastier as you go.

19 coworkers, deck building

Excel Master, Seen-Not-Replied, Payroll Loafer… build job-group synergies.

Events & achievements

14-day launch event, daily check-in rewards, Play Games achievements and leaderboard.

3 languages

Korean, English and Japanese.
