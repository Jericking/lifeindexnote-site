Kaltoe Defense

One line: dodge the boss and clock out at 6 PM sharp in a random merge defense game. ("Kaltoe" is Korean slang for leaving work right on time.)

Hire coworkers, merge two identical ones to promote them (choosing one of three candidates each time), and hold off the flood of urgent emails and meeting invites. Every company has weekday bosses; beat the Friday boss to job-hop to the next one. 8 companies so far, with a year-end event starting December 4.

Ghost Mode

One line: escape group chat overload with an app that replies for you.

When your social battery is empty, turn on Ghost Mode and it replies through the Reply action of the messenger notifications you choose. Premium reads 13 situations and includes dates and times like "tomorrow 7pm", and after each auto-reply suggests 3 replies that fit the message (written by the on-device AI on some recent phones). A fake call and excuse lines help you slip away.
